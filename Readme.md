🎨 Meng To — Digital Urban Sketchbook
An interactive web showcase and digital sketchbook concept created for Meng To (designer, AI educator, and founder of Design+Code). The site bridges traditional architectural sketching with modern web interaction, presenting ink and watercolor studies of Singapore’s urban landscape.

✨ Key Features
📖 Interactive 3D Sketchbook: Physics-based page-turning mechanics that simulate flipping through a real physical art journal.

🔍 Magnifying Glass Inspection Tool: Floating, draggable loupe tool allowing users to examine fine ink linework and watercolor washes at high zoom.

🏛️ Curated Plates Directory: Numbered navigational index organizing illustrations by landmark name and district (e.g., 01 Marina Bay Sands — BAYFRONT).

🎨 Tactile Design System: Subtle botanical shadows, warm paper textures, and custom typography combining classic serif headers with clean sans-serif accents.

🎯 Design Philosophy
The project highlights the intersection of craft and code:

"Teaching designers to build, and builders to see. This sketchbook is the other half of that — the city looked at slowly, in ink and a little colour: shophouse shutters, hawker tents, the bay at dusk."
